Privacy Policy

Last updated: Today

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any of the text you enter into our Word Counter tool. All text analysis, character counting, and keyword density calculations are performed locally in your web browser using JavaScript.

2. Cookies and Advertising

We use third-party advertising companies, such as Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

3. Web Speech API (Dictation)

If you use the Voice Dictation feature, your browser may send your voice audio to your browser provider (e.g., Google for Chrome) for speech-to-text translation according to your browser's own privacy policies. We do not store this audio.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@easywordcounter.vercel.app.
