About Easy Word Counter

Welcome to Easy Word Counter, your number one source for all things related to text analysis, character counting, and writing statistics. We're dedicated to giving you the very best of writing tools, with a focus on speed, privacy, and accuracy.

Our Mission

Founded in 2026, Easy Word Counter was built to solve a simple problem: most online word counters are slow, bloated with heavy frameworks, and send your private text to external servers. We wanted to build something better. Our tool is built with pure Vanilla JavaScript, meaning 100% of the text processing happens instantly in your own browser.

Who We Serve

We serve thousands of users daily, from college students trying to hit exact essay requirements, to SEO professionals optimizing their articles for keyword density, to public speakers estimating their speaking time. Whether you need to check the readability level of your thesis or just want to quickly capitalize a sentence, our tool is designed to be your all-in-one writing companion.

Commitment to Privacy

We believe your words belong to you. Because our tool does not use a backend database to process your text, it is completely impossible for us to save, read, or steal the content you paste into our editor. Your data is perfectly secure.

We hope you enjoy using our tool as much as we enjoyed building it for you. If you have any questions or comments, please don't hesitate to contact us.
