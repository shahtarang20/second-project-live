How to Calculate Speech and Reading Time

By Editorial Team • 6 Min Read

Whether you are preparing a toast for a wedding, a 15-minute presentation for work, or simply writing a blog post, knowing exactly how long it takes to consume your text is an incredibly valuable metric.

The Science of WPM (Words Per Minute)

The calculation for reading and speaking time is entirely based on the concept of Words Per Minute (WPM). While every individual is different, scientists and linguists have calculated the global averages for both reading silently and speaking out loud.

  • Average Reading Speed: The average adult reads silently at roughly 225 to 250 words per minute.
  • Average Speaking Speed: When speaking out loud clearly (such as during a presentation or speech), the average person speaks at about 130 to 150 words per minute.

Why is Speaking Slower than Reading?

You might be surprised to learn that speaking is almost half as fast as reading. When you read silently, your brain groups words together and skips over smaller, less important words. When speaking, however, you have to physically articulate every single syllable, take breaths, and add pauses for dramatic effect or clarity.

How to Calculate Your Time Manually

If you want to calculate how long your speech will take manually, the math is simple:

Total Words ÷ 130 = Total Minutes of Speech

For example, if you have written a 650-word speech, 650 divided by 130 equals exactly 5 minutes.
