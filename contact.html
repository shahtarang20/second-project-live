What to Include in Your Message

A little detail goes a long way toward a fast, useful reply:

  • Bug reports: the browser and device you're using (e.g. "Chrome on Windows" or "Safari on iPhone"), what you did right before the issue appeared, and what you expected to happen instead.
  • Feature requests: what you're trying to accomplish, not just the feature itself — knowing the underlying goal helps us design something that actually solves it.
  • Content corrections: a link to the specific article or guide, and the section where you spotted the issue.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is this tool really free? Yes, the tool is 100% free to use for both personal and commercial purposes.
  • Can I use it offline? Currently, you need an internet connection to load the initial page, but once loaded, the processing happens offline in your browser.
  • Do you save my text? No. We do not have a database and your text is never sent to our servers.
  • I found a bug — how fast will it get fixed? It depends on severity. Issues that break core counting are prioritized immediately; smaller cosmetic issues are batched into regular updates.
  • Can I suggest a topic for a future article? Absolutely — email us the topic and, if you have one, the specific question you'd want it to answer.
