Why use a Free Word and Character Counter?

Whether you are writing a college essay, a blog post, or a tweet, knowing your exact word count and character count is crucial. Our free online word counter tool allows you to instantly check your text statistics without downloading any software. Simply type, paste, or dictate your text into the editor above, and we calculate everything in real-time right inside your browser.

What features does this text analyzer offer?

  • Live Word & Character Count: Instantly see how many words and characters (with or without spaces) you have written.
  • Keyword Density Checker: Perfect for SEO professionals. We automatically scan your text and extract the most used keywords, ignoring common stop words like "the" or "and".
  • Reading & Speaking Time: Planning a speech? Our tool calculates exactly how long it will take to read or speak your text out loud based on average human speeds.
  • Voice Dictation: Tired of typing? Click the "Dictate" button and use your microphone to turn your speech into text.
  • Flesch-Kincaid Readability: We analyze your syllables and sentences to tell you the education level required to understand your writing (from elementary school to college graduate).

Is my data secure?

Yes! 100% of the text processing happens locally in your own web browser. We do not have a database, and your text is never sent to our servers. When you close the tab, your text is gone forever, ensuring complete privacy.
