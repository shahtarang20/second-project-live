How Many Words Per Page? The Complete Conversion Guide

By Editorial Team • 6 Min Read

"Write a 5-page paper" is one of the most common — and most confusing — instructions a student or freelance writer will ever receive. A page isn't a fixed unit of text; it changes completely depending on your font, spacing, and margins. Before you start writing, it helps to know roughly how many words you're actually being asked for.

The Standard Assumption: Double-Spaced, 12pt Times New Roman

Unless told otherwise, most academic and professional style guides (MLA, APA, Chicago) assume 12-point Times New Roman, double-spaced, with 1-inch margins. Under these settings, one page holds approximately 250 words. This is the figure teachers, professors, and word-count calculators use by default, so it's the safest number to plan around.

Single-Spaced Pages

Drop the double spacing and that same page holds roughly 500 to 550 words — almost exactly double. Business reports, cover letters, and internal memos are usually single-spaced, so don't assume a "one-page resume" means 250 words; it typically means closer to 500.

Font Size Changes Everything

Font size has an outsized effect on page count. Compared to the 250-word baseline at 12pt:

  • 11pt: roughly 280–300 words per double-spaced page
  • 12pt: roughly 250 words per double-spaced page (the standard)
  • 14pt: roughly 200–210 words per double-spaced page

If an assignment specifies both a page count and a font, use that font's ratio rather than the generic 250-word rule — a 14pt requirement can add a full page's worth of extra writing to a 10-page paper.

1 page250 words
2 pages500 words
3 pages750 words

Novel Manuscripts: A Different Standard

Publishers don't count words per formatted page — they use a fixed estimate of roughly 250 to 300 words per manuscript page, regardless of how it's typeset for print. A "300-page novel" usually refers to roughly 75,000 to 90,000 words, which is why literary agents almost always ask for a word count rather than a page count when you query them.

Screenplays Are Not Word-Based At All

If you're writing a script, forget word counts entirely. Industry convention treats one properly formatted screenplay page as roughly one minute of screen time, regardless of word density — a page of rapid dialogue and a page of a single wide shot description can both represent one minute. Page count, not word count, is the relevant metric here.
